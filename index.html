I build AI systems that help companies remember, understand, and act on what is happening inside their organization.

My current work is focused on Exoself: an ambient memory layer for companies.

Exoself

Exoself captures and structures the scattered context of a business so leaders and teams can work from a shared, living memory.

It connects decisions, conversations, commitments, client situations, internal frictions, processes, and risks across people, tools, and time.

For founders, managers, and executives, this creates a form of operational ubiquity. They can become aware of important situations they were not directly involved in: a blocked decision, a recurring tension inside a team, friction with a strategic client, or an emerging risk that would normally stay hidden until it becomes costly.

By making company context continuously available and actionable, Exoself reduces organizational friction, improves coordination, and creates leverage for team productivity.

Background

My background combines a PhD in Computer Science, with research in agent-based modeling, reinforcement learning, and game theory; engineering training in nuclear energy at Arts et Métiers ParisTech and the CEA, France's national research organization for energy and nuclear technologies; and years building applied AI products across mobility, environmental assessment, data privacy, and professional operations.
